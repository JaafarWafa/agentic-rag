# Disciplinary Procedure
People Team - CONFIDENTIAL - for HR and managers handling a case

## 1. Principles
The disciplinary procedure is designed to correct behaviour, not to punish. Every case is handled fairly, consistently and confidentially. Employees have the right to be informed of the allegations and to respond before any decision is made.

## 2. Informal Resolution
Minor issues of conduct or performance are first addressed informally by the line manager in a private conversation. The manager records the date and content of the conversation in a short note kept for 6 months.

## 3. Investigation
If the issue is serious or repeated, HR appoints an investigator who is not the employee's line manager. The investigation is completed within 15 working days. The employee may be suspended on full pay during the investigation if their presence could compromise it.

## 4. Disciplinary Hearing
The employee is invited in writing to a disciplinary hearing with at least 5 working days' notice. The employee may be accompanied by a colleague or a staff representative of their choice.

## 5. Sanctions
The possible sanctions, in order of severity, are: a verbal warning, a written warning, a final written warning, and dismissal. A verbal warning stays on file for 6 months, a written warning for 12 months and a final written warning for 18 months. Gross misconduct, such as theft, violence or serious breaches of safety rules, can lead to dismissal without prior warnings.

## 6. Appeals
The employee can appeal any sanction in writing within 10 working days of receiving the decision. The appeal is heard by a manager more senior than the one who made the original decision, and the appeal decision is final.

## 7. Records
All disciplinary records are stored in the restricted section of the HR system. Access is limited to HR business partners and the HR Director.
