DATA BREACH RESPONSE PROCEDURE
Owner: Legal & Compliance   |   Classification: RESTRICTED


1. SCOPE

This procedure applies to any confirmed or suspected breach of personal data held by
Atlas Freight, including customer, employee and supplier data.


2. RESPONSE TEAM

The Breach Response Team is chaired by the Data Protection Officer (DPO) and includes
the CISO, the General Counsel, the Head of Communications and the owner of the
affected system. The team must convene within 4 hours of a breach being confirmed.


3. ASSESSMENT

The team assesses the nature of the data, the number of people affected, and the
likely consequences for them. Every breach, even a minor one, is recorded in the
breach register maintained by the DPO.


4. NOTIFICATION OF THE AUTHORITY

If the breach is likely to result in a risk to individuals, the DPO notifies the
national data protection authority within 72 hours of becoming aware of it. If the
full information is not yet available, an initial notification is made within the
72 hours and completed later.


5. NOTIFICATION OF INDIVIDUALS

Where the breach is likely to result in a high risk to individuals, the affected
people are informed without undue delay, in clear language, with advice on how to
protect themselves. The Head of Communications approves the wording.


6. EVIDENCE PRESERVATION

Logs, affected devices and system images must be preserved. No system may be wiped
or rebuilt before the CISO confirms that forensic images have been taken.


7. POST-INCIDENT REVIEW

A post-incident review is completed within 30 days of closing the breach, and the
lessons learned are presented to the Executive Committee.
