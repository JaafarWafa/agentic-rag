# Compensation Framework 2026

CONFIDENTIAL — HR ONLY. Do not share outside the People Team.

## Salary Bands

Annual gross base salary ranges for 2026, in MAD:

- Band A (operators, drivers, warehouse associates): 60,000 to 96,000
- Band B (coordinators, senior drivers, team leads): 96,000 to 150,000
- Band C (specialists, analysts, engineers): 150,000 to 260,000
- Band D (managers): 260,000 to 420,000
- Band E (directors): 420,000 to 750,000

New hires are normally placed between the minimum and the midpoint of their band. Offers above the midpoint require approval from the HR Director.

## Annual Salary Review

Salaries are reviewed once a year, in April. The 2026 merit budget is 4.5% of the total payroll. Increases are based on the performance rating:

- Rating 5 (exceptional): 7% to 10%
- Rating 4 (strong): 4% to 6%
- Rating 3 (solid): 2% to 3.5%
- Ratings 1 and 2: no merit increase

Promotions to a higher band come with an increase of at least 8%.

## Variable Pay

Band C and above are eligible for an annual bonus. Target bonuses are 8% of base salary for Band C, 12% for Band D and 20% for Band E. The actual payout ranges from 0% to 150% of target depending on company results and individual performance, and is paid in March.

Drivers in Band A and B receive a monthly safety bonus of 600 MAD for each month without an at-fault incident.

## Pay Equity

The People Team runs a pay equity audit every year in January. Any unexplained gender pay gap above 3% within a band triggers a corrective adjustment in the April review.
