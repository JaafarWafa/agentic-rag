# Litigation Hold Procedure

Owner: Legal Department. RESTRICTED — legal staff only.

## When a Hold is Issued

The Legal department issues a litigation hold as soon as litigation, a regulatory investigation or an audit is reasonably anticipated — not only once a claim is formally filed. The General Counsel or a Senior Legal Counsel decides whether a hold is needed within 2 business days of becoming aware of the matter.

## Scope of a Hold

A hold covers all documents and data that may be relevant to the matter, in any format: email, chat messages, shipment records, GPS logs from the fleet telematics system, contracts, and files on laptops and shared drives.

## Notifying Custodians

Legal sends a written hold notice to each custodian, meaning any employee likely to hold relevant information. Custodians must acknowledge the notice within 3 business days. Legal follows up with any custodian who has not acknowledged, and escalates to the custodian's director after 5 business days.

IT must suspend automatic deletion for the mailboxes and systems in scope within 24 hours of receiving the hold notice.

## Obligations During a Hold

Custodians must not delete, alter or move any data covered by the hold, even if normal retention rules would allow deletion. Departing employees who are custodians must have their mailbox and laptop preserved before their account is closed.

## Reminders and Release

Legal sends reminder notices to custodians every 6 months while a hold is active. A hold can only be released in writing by the General Counsel once the matter is fully resolved, including any appeal period.
