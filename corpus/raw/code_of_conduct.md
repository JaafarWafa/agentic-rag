# Atlas Freight Code of Conduct

This Code applies to all employees, directors, contractors and temporary staff. It is published on our public website.

## Gifts and Hospitality

Employees may accept gifts or hospitality from business partners only if the value is under 500 MAD per occasion and the total from the same partner stays under 1,500 MAD per year. Any gift above these limits must be politely declined or handed to the Compliance team.

Cash, vouchers and gift cards must never be accepted, whatever their value.

All gifts and invitations received above 200 MAD must be recorded in the gifts register within 5 working days.

## Conflicts of Interest

Employees must disclose any situation where their personal interests could conflict with those of Atlas Freight, such as a family member working for a supplier or a financial stake in a competitor. Disclosures are made to the Compliance team using the conflict of interest form.

Employees may not take a second job with a competitor, customer or supplier without written approval from the Compliance team.

## Respect and Anti-Harassment

Atlas Freight does not tolerate harassment, discrimination or bullying of any kind. This includes sexual harassment, offensive jokes and exclusion based on gender, origin, religion, disability, age or any other protected characteristic.

Employees who experience or witness harassment should report it to their manager, to the People Team, or through the ethics hotline. Reports are investigated within 10 working days.

## Speaking Up (Whistleblowing)

Concerns about fraud, corruption, safety or any breach of this Code can be raised anonymously through the ethics hotline, available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, by phone or through the online form at ethics.atlasfreight.example.

Atlas Freight prohibits retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith. Retaliation is itself a disciplinary offence.

## Anti-Bribery

Employees must never offer, promise or give anything of value to a public official or a business partner to obtain an improper advantage. Facilitation payments are prohibited, even where they are customary.

## Breaches of the Code

Breaches of this Code can lead to disciplinary action up to and including dismissal.
