# Atlas Freight — Frequently Asked Questions

## About us

Atlas Freight SARL is a (fictional) road and sea freight company founded in 2009, with its head office in Casablanca and regional hubs in Tangier, Agadir and Marrakech. It employs around 1,200 people.

## Customer service

Our customer service team is available Monday to Saturday from 08:00 to 20:00. Customers can reach us by phone, by email at support@atlasfreight.example, or through the live chat on our website. The average response time for email is under 4 hours.

## Tracking a shipment

Shipments can be tracked online with the 12-character tracking number printed on the waybill. Tracking information is updated every 30 minutes.

## Claims for damaged goods

Claims for damaged or lost goods must be submitted within 7 days of delivery, using the claims form on our website. Photos of the damaged packaging and goods must be attached. Claims are answered within 21 days.

## Careers

Open positions are published on our careers page. Applicants hear back within 3 weeks of the closing date. Atlas Freight runs a paid summer internship programme of 8 weeks every July and August.

## Sustainability

Atlas Freight aims to cut its fleet's CO2 emissions by 40% by 2030 compared to 2020. Since 2024, all new urban delivery vans purchased are electric.
