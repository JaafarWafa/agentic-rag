Remote Work Policy

Owner: People Team · Last reviewed: March 2026

Eligibility

Employees in office-based roles are eligible for hybrid work after completing their probation period. Warehouse, fleet and on-site customer roles are not eligible because the work must be performed on site.

Hybrid schedule

Eligible employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week. Tuesday and Thursday are anchor days on which all team members are expected in the office.

Fully remote arrangements are exceptional and require approval from both the department head and the People Team. They are reviewed every 6 months.

Working from abroad

Employees may work from another country for a maximum of 20 working days per calendar year, subject to manager approval submitted at least 15 days in advance. Working from abroad is not allowed in countries under international sanctions.

Equipment and allowance

  • The company provides a laptop, a headset and, on request, one external monitor.
  • Hybrid employees receive a home-office allowance of 250 MAD per month to cover internet and electricity costs.
  • Company equipment remains company property and must be returned within 5 working days of leaving the company.

Security when working remotely

Remote employees must connect through the company VPN when accessing internal systems. Working from public Wi-Fi without the VPN is prohibited. Screens must be locked whenever the device is left unattended.
