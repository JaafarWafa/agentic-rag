# Travel and Expense Policy
Finance Department - Policy FIN-004 - Effective 1 February 2026

## 1. Booking Travel
All business travel must be booked through the company travel desk, at least 14 days before departure for international trips and 5 days before departure for domestic trips. Bookings made outside the travel desk are only reimbursed with the written approval of the Finance Director.

## 2. Air Travel
Economy class is the standard for all flights. Business class is allowed only for flights longer than 6 hours of continuous flying time, and requires approval from a member of the Executive Committee. Employees should choose the lowest logical fare available within two hours of their preferred departure time.

## 3. Ground Transport
Train travel is preferred for journeys between Casablanca, Rabat, Kenitra and Tangier. Employees who use their personal car for business are reimbursed at 3.50 MAD per kilometre, in addition to motorway tolls and parking. Taxis and ride-hailing services are reimbursed when public transport is not practical.

## 4. Accommodation
Hotel costs are reimbursed up to a nightly cap of 1,200 MAD in Morocco and 180 EUR abroad, including breakfast. Higher rates in cities with exceptional prices must be approved in advance by the manager.

## 5. Meals and Per Diem
For travel with an overnight stay, employees receive a daily allowance (per diem) of 400 MAD in Morocco and 60 EUR abroad to cover meals and small expenses. Receipts are not required for the per diem. Meals with clients are reimbursed separately on presentation of an itemised receipt, and the names of the attendees must be recorded on the expense claim.

## 6. Alcohol and Non-Reimbursable Expenses
Alcohol, personal entertainment, fines, traffic violations, minibar charges and loyalty-programme upgrades are never reimbursed.

## 7. Submitting Expense Claims
Expense claims must be submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the end of the trip, with digital copies of all receipts above 100 MAD. Claims submitted after 90 days are rejected. Approved claims are reimbursed with the next monthly payroll.

## 8. Corporate Cards
Employees who travel more than four times per year can request a corporate card. Corporate cards may not be used for personal expenses, even if the employee intends to repay them. Card statements must be reconciled in the expense tool every month.
