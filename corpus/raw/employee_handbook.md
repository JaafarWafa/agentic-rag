# Atlas Freight Employee Handbook

Version 4.2 — effective 1 January 2026. Atlas Freight SARL is a fictional logistics company; this handbook is sample data for the RAG demo.

## 1. Working Hours

The standard working week is 44 hours, Monday to Friday, with core hours from 09:30 to 16:00. Employees agree their start time with their manager between 07:30 and 09:30.

Overtime must be approved in writing by a manager before it is worked. Approved overtime is paid at 125% of the hourly rate on weekdays and 150% on weekends and public holidays. Unapproved overtime is not paid.

Warehouse staff work in three rotating shifts. Shift schedules are published at least 14 days in advance on the HR portal.

## 2. Annual Leave

Full-time employees accrue 1.5 working days of paid annual leave per month of service, which is 18 working days per year. After five years of continuous service, the entitlement increases by 1.5 days for each additional block of five years, up to a maximum of 30 days per year.

Leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 10 working days before the first day of leave. Requests for more than 10 consecutive days need the approval of the department head.

Up to 5 unused days may be carried over into the next calendar year. Carried-over days expire on 31 March. Unused leave is not paid out except at the end of employment.

## 3. Sick Leave

Employees who are ill must notify their manager before 10:00 on the first day of absence. A medical certificate is required for any absence longer than 3 consecutive days, and must be submitted within 48 hours of the employee's return.

Atlas Freight pays full salary for the first 10 days of certified sick leave per calendar year. After that, the employee is covered by the health insurance scheme described in the Benefits section.

## 4. Parental Leave

Birth mothers are entitled to 14 weeks of paid maternity leave. The leave can start up to 6 weeks before the expected due date.

The other parent is entitled to 10 working days of paid paternity leave, to be taken within 30 days of the birth or adoption.

Adoptive parents receive the same entitlements as birth parents from the date the child joins the family.

## 5. Public Holidays

The company observes all official national public holidays. The list of holidays for the year is published on the HR portal every December. Employees required to work on a public holiday receive a compensatory day off in addition to the overtime rate.

## 6. Probation

New employees serve a probation period of 3 months for non-managerial roles and 6 months for managerial roles. The probation period can be extended once, by up to the same length, with written notice.

During probation, either party may end the employment with 7 days' notice.

## 7. Benefits

All permanent employees are enrolled in the group health insurance plan from their first day. The plan covers 80% of outpatient costs and 90% of hospitalisation costs. Spouses and children can be added at no extra cost.

Each employee has an annual learning budget of 6,000 MAD for courses, certifications and conference tickets. Requests are made through the HR portal and must be approved by the manager. Unused budget does not carry over.

Employees receive a monthly meal allowance of 700 MAD, paid with the salary.

## 8. Resignation and Notice

Employees who resign must give written notice of 1 month if they have less than 1 year of service, 2 months for 1 to 5 years, and 3 months for more than 5 years. Notice periods for managers are 3 months regardless of tenure.

Final pay, including any unused annual leave, is settled within 30 days of the last working day.
