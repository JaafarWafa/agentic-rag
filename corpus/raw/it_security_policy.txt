ATLAS   FREIGHT   -   INFORMATION   SECURITY   POLICY
Document ID: SEC-001        Classification: Internal        Rev. 7


PURPOSE

This policy sets the minimum security rules for everyone who uses Atlas Freight
systems, devices or data, including contractors.


PASSWORDS AND AUTHENTICATION

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Passphrases made of several
words are encouraged.   Passwords must never be reused across systems and must
never be shared, including with IT staff.

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for email, the VPN, the HR portal
and all cloud applications. Hardware security keys are issued to administrators.

Passwords are not rotated on a schedule. They must be changed immediately if
there is any suspicion that they have been exposed.


DEVICES

Company laptops are encrypted with full-disk encryption and managed by IT.
Software may only be installed from the company software catalogue. Personal
devices may access email only through the approved mobile app, and never
connect to the internal network.

Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the IT Service Desk within 1 hour of
discovery, by phone on extension 4444 or by email to security@atlasfreight.example.


PHISHING AND SUSPICIOUS EMAIL

Do not click links or open attachments in unexpected emails. Report suspicious
emails with the "Report Phishing" button in Outlook. The security team reviews
every report within 4 business hours.

Do not click links or open attachments in unexpected emails. Report suspicious
emails with the "Report Phishing" button in Outlook. The security team reviews
every report within 4 business hours.

Every employee completes the phishing awareness training at onboarding and then
once per year. Simulated phishing campaigns are run every quarter.


DATA CLASSIFICATION

Data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. Customer
shipment data and personal data are Confidential. Payroll and legal case files
are Restricted. Restricted data may not be stored on removable media or sent to
personal email accounts.


INCIDENT REPORTING

Any suspected security incident must be reported to the IT Service Desk
immediately. Employees must not attempt to investigate an incident themselves.
The handling of confirmed personal-data breaches is governed by the separate
Data Breach Response Procedure, owned by the Legal department.


EXCEPTIONS

Exceptions to this policy must be approved in writing by the Chief Information
Security Officer and are valid for a maximum of 12 months.
